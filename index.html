Frequently Asked Questions

What curriculum does Nalapad Academy follow?
Nalapad Academy follows the internationally accredited Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) curriculum from Early Years (EYFS) through Cambridge Primary, Lower Secondary, IGCSE, and AS & A Levels (School Code: IA031).
What is the student-teacher ratio in classrooms?
We maintain an optimal ratio of 6:1 to 15:1 depending on grade levels, ensuring individual attention, personalized learning plans, and close academic tracking for every child.
Are campus visits allowed during working hours?
Yes! We welcome parents for campus visits Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. You can pre-book a slot via our "Book Campus Visit" button or by calling +91 91208 88888.
Do you provide school transport facilities?
Yes, we provide GPS-tracked, air-conditioned bus transportation covering major areas across Indiranagar, Domlur, Koramangala, Whitefield, MG Road, and Old Airport Road with dedicated female bus attendants.
